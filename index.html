How this is ranked

For every matchup this week, the model projects a score (same offense-vs-defense math as Matchup) and compares it to the actual market spread/total from the odds board. These are the games where that gap is biggest — where the model disagrees with the market the most, in either direction. A big gap isn't automatically right; it just means the numbers loaded here see it very differently than the number your book is offering. Needs both team stats and a market line loaded to show up.
